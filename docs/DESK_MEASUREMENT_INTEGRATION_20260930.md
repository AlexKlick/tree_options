The scoring contract is `split-local-own-rate/v2`. Held-out decisions must not
change tune ranking through participation or horizon selection. The default
minimum is 30 distinct evaluated decision boards. Repeated copies of the same
decision do not multiply coverage; duplicate snapshot identities are rejected.
The floor and scoring version are registered inputs. Existing A/A, Holm,
incumbent, stability and purge requirements continue to apply. Re-scoring old
receipts is descriptive, even when the new arithmetic is favorable.

The legacy shared-null comparison remains labeled separately. Its **point
estimate** is net minus a shared constant; this does not imply that confidence
interval lower-bound ordering is identical to net ordering.

The optional cost surface combines reported delta and DTE marginals into derived
cells. It supplies no jointly measured calibration, intraday quote, authoritative
fill price or observed fee. A full quoted spread is halved once; both package legs
pay modeled opening and closing costs. Missing leg classification or future
availability refuses the complete package. Those refusals retain identities and
reasons instead of silently becoming zero profit. September 2026 EOD calibration
cannot establish cost availability for September 2025 decisions. This path stays
retrospective sensitivity and cannot earn confirmatory review eligibility.

The cockpit distinguishes historical/descriptive scores from the registered
contract, displays own-rate versus legacy comparisons and the participation floor,
and exposes `NO_PRICE`/`DATA_GATED` outcomes. It requires strict true rule checks
and consistent numeric evidence before displaying review eligibility. This is an
operator review label, never an order or deployment authorization.
